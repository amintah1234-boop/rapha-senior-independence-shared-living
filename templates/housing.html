INDEPENDENCE & PERSONAL CHOICE

Independent Living

Your Life, Your Choices

  • We respect every resident’s right to make their own decisions and manage their daily lives.
  • Our community is designed for seniors who can take care of their personal needs and daily activities independently.
  • Residents are responsible for their personal care, medications, healthcare, and everyday routines.
  • We provide a shared housing environment where residents can enjoy their independence while living among others.

STAYING CONNECTED

Transportation and Community Resources

We understand that getting to appointments, shopping, and community activities can sometimes be difficult.

We can help residents find information about transportation services and arrange transportation through available outside providers.

Transportation may be arranged for:

  • Doctor appointments.
  • Grocery shopping.
  • Personal errands.
  • Senior center activities.
  • Social outings.

Our goal is to help residents stay connected with their community.

A SHARED LIVING COMMUNITY

Who Can Live Here?

Our community is designed for seniors who:

  • Want comfortable shared housing.
  • Enjoy living with other people.
  • Can manage their personal care independently.
  • Want to maintain their freedom and privacy.
  • Enjoy social activities and companionship.
  • Are comfortable sharing household spaces.
  • Want a comfortable place to call home.

A PLACE TO CONNECT AND BELONG

Why Choose Our Community?

Finding a good place to live is important. We want seniors and their families to feel comfortable choosing our community.

We offer:

  • Shared housing.
  • Comfortable bedrooms.
  • Shared household spaces.
  • Meal options.
  • Social activities.
  • Game nights and entertainment.
  • Opportunities to make friends.
  • A welcoming and respectful environment.
  • Freedom and independence.

We offer more than a place to live. We offer a place to connect, share, and belong.
