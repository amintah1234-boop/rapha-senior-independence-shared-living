About Us

Helping seniors enjoy independent living through comfortable shared housing, friendship, and community.

OUR COMMUNITY

A place to feel
respected and at home.

Our community was created to give seniors a comfortable place to live.

We believe older adults should have the opportunity to enjoy life, meet new people, and maintain their independence.

Our shared living home provides a welcoming environment where residents can enjoy their privacy while also having opportunities to socialize and make friends.

We want everyone who lives in our community to feel respected, valued, and at home.
