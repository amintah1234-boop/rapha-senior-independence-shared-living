FRIENDSHIP & BELONGING

Welcome to Rapha Senior Independence Shared Living

  • We provide a friendly and comfortable shared living environment for older adults who want to enjoy independent living without living alone.
  • We understand that living alone can sometimes be lonely and expensive. Our goal is to give seniors another housing option where they can enjoy comfort, friendship, and a sense of belonging.
  • Residents can enjoy their own personal space while sharing common areas, meals, and activities with others.
  • Our community is built on respect, kindness, independence, and friendship.
  • We believe everyone deserves a comfortable home and a place where they feel welcome.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is senior independent shared living?

It is a housing option where seniors live together, share common spaces, and maintain their own independence.

Do residents have their own bedrooms?

Private or shared bedrooms may be available depending on the home’s layout and availability.

Are meals available?

Meal arrangements may be available depending on the housing package.

Are activities provided?

Yes. Activities may include game nights, birthday celebrations, and social gatherings.

Do residents receive personal care?

No. Residents are responsible for their own personal care and daily needs.

How can I apply?

Contact us to learn about available rooms, rental costs, and how to schedule a visit.
