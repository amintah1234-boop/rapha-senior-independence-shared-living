A COMFORTABLE PLACE TO CALL HOME

Housing &
Amenities

We want every resident to feel comfortable, supported, and genuinely at home.

Rapha encourages independence, connection, and a sense of belonging in a calm, welcoming shared living environment.

A PLACE WITH ROOM TO FEEL AT HOME

Our housing may include:

  • •Private bedrooms
  • •Comfortable living rooms
  • •Shared kitchen and dining areas
  • •Laundry facilities
  • • Utilities and internet Depending on the rental agreement.
  • •Outdoor spaces for relaxation
  • •Shared household amenities
  • •24/7 Staff Support

Utilities and internet availability may vary depending on the rental agreement.

Our goal is to provide a clean, comfortable, and welcoming place that residents can call home.

INDEPENDENCE & PERSONAL CHOICE

Independent Living

Your Life, Your Choices

  • We respect each resident’s right to make their own decisions and manage daily life on their own terms.
  • Our community is designed for seniors who can manage their personal needs and daily routines independently.
  • Residents remain responsible for their personal care, medications, healthcare, and everyday routines.
  • We provide a shared housing environment where residents can enjoy independence while living in a supportive community.

STAYING CONNECTED

Transportation and Community Resources

We understand that getting to appointments, shopping, and community activities can sometimes be difficult.

We can help residents explore transportation options and connect with available outside providers when needed.

Transportation may be arranged for:

  • Medical appointments.
  • Grocery shopping.
  • Personal errands.
  • Senior center activities.
  • Social outings.

Our goal is to help residents remain active, connected, and confident in their daily routines.

A SHARED LIVING COMMUNITY

Who Can Live Here?

Our community is designed for seniors who:

  • Want a comfortable, supportive place to live.
  • Enjoy living with others in a respectful community.
  • Can manage their personal care independently.
  • Value privacy while still enjoying connection and companionship.
  • Enjoy social activities and regular community interaction.
  • Are comfortable sharing household spaces.
  • Want a place that feels like home.

A PLACE TO CONNECT AND BELONG

Why Choose Our Community?

Finding a good place to live is an important decision. We want seniors and their families to feel confident and comfortable exploring our community.

We offer:

  • Shared housing with a warm, welcoming atmosphere.
  • Comfortable personal spaces with private bedrooms.
  • Shared household areas for daily living.
  • Meal arrangements and social dining opportunities.
  • Community activities and gatherings.
  • Arts and crafts, movie nights, and bingo.
  • Opportunities to build friendships.
  • A respectful, supportive environment.
  • Freedom, privacy, and independence.

We offer more than a place to live. We offer a place to connect, share, and belong.
